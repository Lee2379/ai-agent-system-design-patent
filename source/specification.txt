Field of the Invention:
This invention relates to the field of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and autonomous drone
technology. Specifically, it describes how AI and autonomous drone systems can address
logistics and delivery challenges in East Asian countries focused on South Korea, Japan,
and China.
This invention provides a smart drone delivery system which utilizes a multi-agent AI
framework, satellite communication, and GPS-based tracking to enable safe, efficient, and
environmentally sustainable delivery operations across regions in South Korea, Japan, and
China. The system integrates hierarchical and network AI agents to manage drone
operations, optimize routing, monitor safety, and communicate with users in real time.

Background:
Current delivery systems in East Asian countries depend heavily on human drivers and
vehicles powered by fossil fuels, which are neither cost-efficient nor environmentally
sustainable. These traditional systems result in high operational costs, contributing to
carbon emissions, and often fail to reach customers in geographically difficult areas such
as mountainous, rural areas.

In East Asian countries, the combination of dense urban centers and complex terrain
further increases delivery challenges. Large portions of South Korea are mountainous,
while Japan’s dispersed geography makes human-based delivery labor-intensive and
expensive. In China, there is also a need for autonomous delivery systems to enhance
delivery efficiency. Additionally, due to an ongoing labor shortage, these countries lack
sufficient human resources to provide cost-efficient delivery services to suburban and
rural areas.
Despite few drone delivery systems have been previously developed, most lack
autonomous adaptability, real-time coordination, and scalable AI management, which are
core aspects of autonomous delivery system. They rely mainly on centralized control
systems without intelligent decision-making capabilities at the drone or agent level. Thus,
their efficiency and reliability are limited in dynamic real-world logistics environments.
To address these limitations, this invention proposes a multi-agent AI-based drone
delivery system capable of learning, coordinating, and performing delivery tasks
autonomously, which are cardinal facet of autonomous delivery system. This invention
aims to reduce costs, save time, alleviate traffic congestion, and promote environmentally
friendly logistics through the use of AI coordination and electric-powered drones in
South Korea, Japan, and China.

Summary of the Invention:
The invention provides a smart drone delivery system that integrates AI agents, satellite
communication, and GPS to manage autonomous delivery operations. The system
employs a multi-agent structure consisting of:
 Hierarchical AI Agents, responsible for overall control, scheduling, and
coordination of drone fleets.
 Network AI Agents, responsible for real-time communication, data exchange, and
cooperation among drones and customers.
System Operation:
1. A customer places an order via a connected delivery platform.
2. The AI agent determines the customer’s real-time location and assigns an
available drone.
3. The drone navigates autonomously using GPS and satellite communication with

continuous guidance from the AI agent.
4. Approximately five minutes before arrival, the AI agent sends a text or
application-based alert notifying the customer of the incoming delivery.
5. The parcel can be safely received at a pre-defined drop-off location such as a
ground-level delivery zone or designated window.
6. All operational data - including flight paths, sensor readings, and images - are
stored and reused to improve AI performance. These data can also be utilized in
autonomous driving, air taxi, and transportation systems for the future.
Key Advantages:
 Cost reduction through automation and elimination of delivery fees.
 Time efficiency from optimized routing and autonomous control.
 Eco-friendly operation using electric power instead of fossil fuels.
 Accessibility to mountainous, rural areas in South Korea and Japan.

Detailed Description:
System Architecture:
The system comprises a central AI control server linked to autonomous drones equipped
with GPS, sensors, cameras, and communication modules. The control server coordinates
operations through hierarchical AI agents that manage tasks and network agents that
facilitate inter-drone communication.
AI Agent Operation:
Each AI agent employs deep learning algorithms for path optimization, obstacle
avoidance, and dynamic route updates based on traffic, weather, and environmental
data. The agents learn continuously from historical delivery information to improve
accuracy and efficiency.
Communication Framework:
The system utilizes satellite communication to ensure high precision and stability.
Network agents manage continuous data exchange between drones, the control center,
and customers for seamless delivery management. Also, for security, the satellite
communication system uses encrypted, authenticated, and quantum-resistant
cryptographic protocols to reduce vulnerability to interference and spoofing, ensuring

secure, reliable drone operations and protecting data against network threats.
Product Delivery Process:
1. The AI system receives a delivery request and determines the optimal drone and
flight path.
2. The assigned drone receives GPS coordinates and maintains real-time
communication with the AI agent.
3. Five minutes before arrival, the AI agent automatically sends a notification
message to the customer. The drone autonomously delivers the package to the
designated location.
Learning and Optimization:
Data collected during each delivery - including images, flight paths, and sensor data -
are processed by the AI system to enhance navigation accuracy, energy efficiency, and
predictive logistics planning. These data are stored in a secure, encrypted cloud
database.
Applications and Extensions:
The system can also be applied to medical supply transport, disaster relief logistics, and
urban air mobility systems such as air taxis. The modular multi-agent design allows
scalability and integration with future intelligent transportation infrastructure.
Safety Considerations:
Safety is prioritized through redundancy in communication links, automatic obstacle
avoidance, and continuous monitoring. Each drone is equipped with object-detection
sensors enabling it to detect nearby humans or vehicles and automatically adjust its
flight path to prevent collisions. Drones are also programmed to return to base or land
safely in the event of power failure or communication loss.

Abstract:
An AI-based smart drone delivery system utilizing multi-agent technology, satellite
communication, and GPS tracking for autonomous parcel delivery for industry. The
invention features hierarchical and network AI agents that manage drone operations,

achieves cost savings, reduced emissions, and enhanced accessibility to urban and
remote areas in South Korea, Japan, and China.
